1. Design System Purpose
This guide locks the visual language for the entire Meridian Residences application. The portal should feel like one
premium residential management platform, not four independently designed pages.
Overall vibe: modern luxury serviced apartment + clean SaaS dashboard.
The interface should communicate premium residential living, trust, clarity, calmness and operational usefulness. It
should be professional and polished without becoming a flashy hotel site, cyber/AI dashboard, overly colorful SaaS
product, or a page full of gradients.
Non-negotiable design principles
 One application: every page shares the same shell, typography, colors, spacing and component language.
 Calm premium feel: forest green, olive, warm gold, warm cream and white; gold is only an accent.
 Functional first: resident tasks such as leases, invoices and maintenance must be immediately understandable.
 Consistency over creativity: individual contributors may add content and workflow-specific controls, but should
not invent new visual systems.
 AI belongs to the product: the assistant may be visually distinctive within the same system, but must not become
a separate ChatGPT clone.
